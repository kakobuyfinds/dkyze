What is a BBDBuy spreadsheet?

A BBDBuy spreadsheet is a product research sheet used to organize candidate item links and comparison notes. Useful fields include the source URL, exact variant, measurements, evidence date and unresolved questions. This page is an independent guide, not an official BBDBuy service.

02 / BBDBuy shopping guide

Build the record
before the shortlist.

The same three-part method works across shoes, jerseys, outerwear and accessories. Treat missing information as an open question, not a confirmed detail.

01

Capture the source.

Store the original product URL, seller name, item identifier and observation date. Keep the full source in your own sheet even when you use a shorter display label.

02

Isolate the variant.

Record the chosen color, size and option text. Where several variants share a listing, use separate rows whenever their measurements, photos or quoted prices differ.

03

Keep the evidence.

Add the relevant photo reference, measurement source and unanswered questions. Revisit the original listing before acting on an old note or comparison.

03 / BBDBuy product comparison

Compare like
with like.

Two rows can look comparable while describing different variants, measuring methods or levels of evidence. Make those differences visible before deciding which item belongs on your shortlist.

Are these the same product options?
Match the selected variant and identifier, not only the title or cover photograph.
Do the measurements describe the same thing?
A flat garment width, a body circumference and a package dimension are different records.
Are the costs being compared on the same basis?
Separate the item quote from delivery, service charges and other costs that may apply.
Which details are still uncertain?
Mark an unconfirmed fabric, missing photo or unclear option explicitly instead of filling the gap by assumption.

05 / Direct answers

Before you
save the row.

Clear definitions help keep a research sheet useful. Confirm service-specific details with the service you intend to use and check the original product source.

Where do the category buttons go?

The category buttons open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. They are browsing shortcuts, not links to an official BBDBuy catalog.

Does a brand reference image verify a seller’s item?

No. A reference image identifies the visual subject being discussed. It does not establish a seller’s stock, authorization, authenticity or the condition of an item.

How should I compare sizes across different listings?

Compare measurements taken using the same method and unit. Keep body measurements separate from garment measurements, and record whether a dimension describes the item, its packaging or a size chart.

Can this page confirm that a product link works with BBDBuy?

No. Check the exact source URL in the service you intend to use and consult its current support information. Compatibility is not tested or guaranteed by this guide.

How often should a product row be checked?

Recheck a row before making a purchase decision and whenever the source listing changes. Record when the information was observed rather than assuming that an older price, photo or option is still current.
